Colleague 360

A short, constructive round of input between colleagues. One person writes about one colleague at a time: what they do well that should continue, what there could be more of, and anything worth acknowledging.

Input is collected by the Performance Support Guide. The colleague it is about never sees who said what, and it is never used in performance reviews, employment decisions or HR processes.

For everyone

Give input

Ten to fifteen minutes, one colleague per submission. Your draft saves as you go, so you can close the tab and come back.

What happens at the end

Print and send

The last page holds everything you have written on one screen, editable in place. When it reads right, print it as a PDF and email that PDF to simon@stperformanceglobal.com. Nothing is sent automatically.

No account, no upload, no file to manage

How the cycle runs

  • Everyone gives input. One colleague per submission, as many as you have something useful to say about.
  • The Support Guide collects it. Each PDF arrives by email and is read as it was written.
  • Nothing goes back below three contributors. If only one or two people write about a colleague, a comment can be traced back to its author by elimination, so the summary waits.
  • Nobody's words are altered. Comments reach the colleague exactly as they were written, grouped by area, with no attribution.
